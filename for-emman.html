Our Story

I could still remember the day we met... on an online platform, apparently. You were posing as Ken Rainer and I was Tomie Ure (Barbie Rainer when we got together). I really thought you were just another person in my chat list, but little did I know you would become someone my heart would never really stop choosing.

We got together. Planned our future together. Called for hours and hours. Slept together virtually—you were from Cebu and I'm from Zamboanga del Sur. Miles apart, I'd say. But it was never just distance for us—it was longing, hope, and a connection that felt deeper than the miles between us.

Years have passed and we eventually got toxic, or perhaps love just left the picture. We tried to fix it, but it just wouldn't work. Maybe we were both healing in different ways, or maybe it was a lesson we had to learn the hard way. But even then, my heart never really stopped loving you.

💭 A Memory That Makes Me Smile

Remember the time when I got so mad because you barely replied? Turns out your mom confiscated your phone because you were grounded, so you only chatted with me using your smart TV. We laughed about it later, and that memory still feels like one of the sweetest reminders of how much we loved each other in the simplest ways.

Now... 2 years after breaking up, I finally saw you. I finally held your hand. I finally kissed you. I finally felt your warmth and got to embrace you. I only dreamt about that in the past, and now it actually happened.

I thought I've fully moved on. I thought two years was enough for me to let go of my first love and first and last love, and yet it wasn't. My love for you is immortal. I will always love you, maybe in a different way now, but still with the same depth and sincerity.

✨ My Favorite Recent Memory ✨

When you accompanied me to ride the bus from IT Park to my hotel and then when I was about to ride it, you stopped me and kissed me. The bus started to move because of what you did, and we both laughed because we were in that moment, with all the world moving around us, but it felt like time had paused just for us. That memory means so much to me.
